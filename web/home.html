Privacy

No cookies. No personal data.

Visitors are counted with a daily-rotating hash and a truncated IP. Raw IPs and cookies are never stored. Rollups are kept per project and isolated by Postgres row-level security.

No consent banner

Nothing is stored on the visitor’s device beyond a per-tab session flag.

Strict isolation

Every query runs under one project’s identity.

Your data stays yours

Self-host from one repository, or use the shared service.
