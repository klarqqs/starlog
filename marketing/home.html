Privacy

Privacy by default. No cookies, no personal data.

Stellog is built on Umami: it collects only anonymous usage data. A linked wallet stores the public address only.

No cookies, no consent banner

Nothing is stored on the visitor’s device beyond a per-tab session flag.

Strict isolation

Every query runs under one project’s identity.

Your data stays yours

Self-host from one repository, or use the shared service.
